Quiet, by design.

Eleven private residences set in forty-two acres of Odisha forest, beside a stream that never stops talking.

We built less, so the forest could stay.

Every residence is raised on piers to leave the roots untouched, built from reclaimed timber, and powered by the sun. Dinner comes from our own farm. There are no televisions, and the wifi is kept to the lounge.

Days with nothing to prove

Forest walk at first light

A resident naturalist leads a ninety-minute loop past the stream and the old sal grove. Tea and binoculars are provided. Included with every stay.

Dinner from the farm

Vegetables picked that afternoon and cooked over wood fire at a single long table. The menu follows the harvest.

Stream-side yoga

Sunrise and sunset sessions on the bamboo deck, for every level. Private sessions can be arranged.

The stargazing deck

Lights go low after nine so the sky can take over. A telescope and a short guided session on clear nights.
